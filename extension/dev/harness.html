Gradient descent, step by step

Gradient descent updates parameters in the direction that most quickly reduces the loss. The learning rate controls how far each step goes.

θt+1 = θt − η ∇L(θt)

Here η is the learning rate and ∇L is the gradient of the loss with respect to the parameters. Too large an η overshoots the minimum; too small makes convergence slow.

for step in range(epochs):
    grad = loss_gradient(theta)
    theta = theta - lr * grad
